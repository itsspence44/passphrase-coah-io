# Passphrase Coach
 

**1. The Client and the Problem**

The client is a company that is concerned about attackers getting into employee accounts and accessing important company information.
The problem is that employees may use weak or easy-to-guess passwords. This can make it easier for attackers to access accounts. The client needs a simple tool that helps employees create longer and stronger passphrases.

**2. Why It Matters**

Password security is important because weak passwords can put company information and employee accounts at risk.

According to the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), passwords should be at least 16 characters long. CISA also recommends using different passwords for different accounts.

_Source: CISA – Four Easy Ways to Stay Safe Online_

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) recommends a minimum of 15 characters for passwords used as a single authentication factor. Longer passwords can make it harder for attackers to guess them.

_Source: NIST – Digital Identity Guidelines_

These facts show why companies should encourage employees to use longer and stronger passphrases.

**3. What the Tool Does**

Passphrase Coach is a Python program that helps users check their passphrases.


**The tool will:**

Ask users to enter a passphrase.

Check whether the passphrase meets the minimum length requirement.

Identify weak or common passphrases using its built-in data.

Give users helpful messages when their passphrase needs improvement.

Allow users to continue checking passphrases until they choose to quit.

The tool will not:

Prevent hackers from accessing company accounts.

Guarantee that a passphrase is completely secure.

Monitor company computers or networks.

Replace other security protections, such as multifactor authentication.
